Bowl Builder

Our Bowl Builder and kitchen ecosystem are designed to automate the makeline. Your team preps ingredients, loads them into thermally controlled dispensers, and walks away. Live order assembly, from bowl dispense to bag close, is handled entirely by Bowl Builder.

Bowl Builder saves up to 50% of labor cost while reducing order defects and increasing total throughput. It helps you solve some of our industry’s hardest challenges, from staffing shortages, to high-stress and high-defect peak times, to shrinking margins and unit growth impediments.
