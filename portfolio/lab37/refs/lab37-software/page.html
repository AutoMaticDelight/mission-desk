02

Order feed

Every order. Effortlessly in sync.
Track, edit, and manage orders in real time—with clarity and control built in. See what’s in progress, what’s held, and what needs attention. Adjust quantities, pause a bowl, or update supply levels with just a few taps. No noise. No confusion. Just a kitchen that runs exactly the way it should.

03

Baseball card

The merchant receipt reimagined. Every bowl gets its own digital fingerprint. Photo. Timestamp. Portion weights. Ingredient breakdown. It’s a snapshot of exactly what was made—and how. So you can see the truth behind every order, with nothing left to question.

04

Intelligent cameras

Eyes on everything. Cameras throughout the system monitor what matters most—from ingredient levels to prep accuracy to line performance. No more second guessing. Just real-time insight, built directly into the flow of your kitchen.

05

Much more..

The system evolves with you.From predictive supply management to multi-robot coordination, the Lab37 platform is built to grow. One unified software layer, designed for what’s now—and what’s next.
