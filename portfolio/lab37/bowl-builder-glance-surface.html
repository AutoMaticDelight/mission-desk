Why this surface works

One glance answers: what is working, what needs me, and what will happen next.

The board makes the full Bowl Builder visible without turning the interface into a wall of telemetry. Every touchpoint uses the same compact structure: status light, physical name, large current measurement, plain-language state, and an action cue only when needed.

Physical location

Every card maps to a real station: cold rail, dispenser, bowl source, verification, packaging, or handoff.

Single primary metric

Temperature, fill, quantity, cycle time, pass rate, or wait time leads—not a generic status label.

Status light + color

Light indicates health from a distance. Amber is reserved for attention; solid color identifies the active focus.

Actionable exception

Only a station needing action exposes an immediate instruction such as reroute, fill, or recalibrate.

Shared state, role views

Operators, prep, managers, technicians, and fleet teams see one truth through different decision lenses.
