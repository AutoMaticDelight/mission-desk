One token source, two skins, one worker HMI

Everything on the worker panel and manager console comes from the tokens on this page. The dark skin and the light skin share every size, radius, spacing value and rule; only the color layer changes. A state is a color first and a number second. Nothing here is decorative.

Logo

The mark is a solid hot yellow-green circle with the icon in black in the middle. The icon is the name: a solid O over a bowl, no outlines anywhere. The circle is the hot of the surface it sits on (#cfff3e on dark, #c8f000 on light); the icon is always ink. The circle has no outline. On a hot surface, drop the circle and use the icon alone in ink. Never add a gradient, never rotate it, never put the icon in any color but ink.

On lightcircle: --l-hot #c8f000 · icon: ink
On darkcircle: --d-hot #cfff3e · icon: ink
On the dockcircle: --d-hot · icon: ink
On hot (icon only)no circle · icon: ink

Semantic first. A worker learns four colors: green is fine, amber is getting low, hot yellow-green is needs you now, and the same yellow-green is the button that fixes it. There is no red anywhere; it fails for color-blind workers under kitchen light.

Components

Buttons · one system

One big verb per alert, never a generic "OK". Secondary actions are glove buttons that open a hold-two-seconds confirm sheet. Nothing in the HMI is a text link.

Machine line · one dot, one word

Bowl 01 14 queued Bowl 02 held Chicken 2 12 open Dispensers 17/18 ConveyorSealerPickupPaymentsNetwork

Alert numbers · the hero on top

4 MINuntil late
7orders waiting
12slots open at Bowl 2

Minutes-until-late is always the middle datum in a scene and always the hero. The other two sit under it. The whole block is centered between the headline column and the button. Dividers are straight lines, never rounded.

Rules (locked)

  • One screen. Fits one iPad, landscape and portrait, 9.7" to 12.9", no scroll. Verified sizes: 1024×768, 1180×820, 1366×1024, 820×1180.
  • Fixed header, floating status card, fixed dock. Both lockups run edge to edge with square corners. Spare height opens between Status and Actions only.
  • HMI, not a web page. No text selection, no double-tap zoom, press feedback everywhere, no text links.
  • No small text. Body ≥14px, labels ≥13px. Bigger, bolder, higher contrast; never cute.
  • Mains bigger than toppings. Proteins and bases lead; five groups by taxonomy; dairy is one box.
  • Photo on every box, left 25%, square-cropped, greyed when held.
  • The progress bar is the message. Percent and refill-by on every box; the forecast walks the Thursday curve to 15%.
  • No red. Needs-you-now is the hot yellow-green of the action button. Low is amber. Fine is green. The alert dot is hot orange.
  • Low or now on light = the box goes dark with bright yellow numbers.
  • Alert card: headline + sub + two glove buttons · centered number stack with the minutes hero · one verb-specific big button.
  • Manager: 100% = on plan. Concept small, not bold. Throughput right-justified, tabular. Store names are real.
  • Done = verified on iPad sizes in the browser before it ships. Rules live in PANEL-DESIGN.md.
