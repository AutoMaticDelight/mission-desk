My first job in college was cooking in the on-campus restaurants, and I spent ten years bartending and catering while I learned design. So I know the order process, the edits, the stock, the vendors, the labor, and what a rush feels like from behind the line. Then twenty-plus years designing tools for people doing real work under pressure: analysts, technicians, teachers, line workers. A hundred-plus shipped products. Your posting asks for three things at once, and I've done all three.

Screens on machines. At iRobot I designed the remote-drive controller for a telepresence robot, a tablet on a mobile base. For Toyota I designed Survive the Drive, a commuter app on the Entune in-vehicle screen. This year I built Bench, a glove-first tablet for technicians on a spacecraft floor that verifies hardware in real time, and I've already mocked up 21 Bowl Builder operator screens to think through your lunch rush.

Internal operational tools. At Istation I built the teacher's learning-management surface from zero, on iPad, for four million students and ninety thousand teachers: a thousand-lesson library, dense lists, standards filtering, one component system across eight products. At Hyundai I sat with recall analysts for three weeks and replaced a rejected dashboard with the workflow they actually ran. At ItsOn I designed a whole phone-service ecosystem, including an intent builder that turns plans and devices into plain conditions an operator drags together. And at AUTH57, my own company, I turned eleven thousand rules into one answer with its citation, with a human in the middle confirming everything the AI proposes.

Food and customers. Four food-ordering apps, including Feed.me, which turns a twenty-tap order into a one-tap yes.

I own the design system and I close the loop in code, Figma to React, so engineers get working states, not comps. I come up to speed fast: a day on Apple's design system, a week on Hyundai's forty-source recall workflow. I'm in Burbank, I want to be onsite five days, and I'd like to see the Bowl Builder in person.
